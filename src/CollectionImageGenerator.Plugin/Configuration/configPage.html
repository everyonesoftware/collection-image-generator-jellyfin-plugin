Collection Image Generator

Create collection collages from up to four local member posters. No remote images are downloaded.

Allow daily, manual and post-scan generation.
The same collection and members produce the same stable-random order. Choose how to fill unused quadrants below.
With two posters, simple repeat gives A B / A B; checkered gives A B / B A. With one or three, checkered matches simple repeat. Do not repeat fills quadrants in reading order and leaves unused space transparent. Applies to usable posters; text overlays still apply.
Use {name} for the collection name. Text is disabled by default.
Maximum 8,388,608 pixels in total.
